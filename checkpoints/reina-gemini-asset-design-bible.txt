CORE IDENTITY

Reina is a dark card world built from:
- Black void.
- Face-down cards in suspended motion.
- Charcoal faceted surfaces.
- Crimson and coral-red emission.
- A repeated eye-like bladed iris seal.
- Nested ornamental card borders.
- Hard clipped interface geometry.
- Cream editorial typography.
- Controlled asymmetry around a strict geometric system.

The mood is secretive, aggressive, ceremonial, high-energy, and precise. Reina is not ancient teal ruin fantasy, cyberpunk, generic occult horror, esports UI, or colorful gacha art.

CARD-BACK DESIGN

Canonical direction matches the supplied red card-back reference and reina-fe card-flip frames.

Physical form:
- Portrait card with standard trading-card proportions.
- Slightly rounded physical corners.
- Thin dark edge.
- Matte charcoal or black card field.
- No orientation-revealing text.

Border system:
- One muted outer perimeter line.
- One brighter inset crimson frame.
- Nested parallel line segments.
- Angular stepped or clipped corner brackets.
- Small inward chevrons, diamonds, or triangular interruptions.
- Narrow crisp emissive cores with restrained red bloom.
- Border remains readable at 58 by 82 pixels.
- Ornament stays subordinate to center emblem.

Center emblem:
- Large radial eye, eclipse, bladed iris, or arcane aperture.
- Near-black circular core.
- Bright segmented crimson ring.
- Four dominant hooked blades or curved points.
- Four secondary radial points.
- Dark outer disc with low-contrast facets.
- Broken concentric tracks rather than a generic target.
- Roughly fourfold or eightfold rotational construction.
- Strict geometric symmetry in shape.
- Asymmetric lighting for depth.

Surface:
- Dark radial facets around emblem.
- Low-contrast folded-plane or fractured-armor geometry.
- Fine restrained grain.
- Matte finish.
- Slight embossed depth.
- No glossy plastic or chrome.

Lighting:
- Card graphics appear internally emissive.
- Brightest emission is concentrated on selected border and emblem edges.
- Adjacent faces remain dark.
- Bloom never closes border gaps or flattens emblem depth.
- Center remains near-black for maximum contrast.

Strict exclusions:
- No text.
- No numbers.
- No character portrait.
- No generic spiral.
- No blue trading-card vortex.
- No rainbow foil.
- No broad gold frame.
- No fantasy runes.
- No decorative language resembling readable letters.
- No full-surface neon outline.

CARD-STORM MOTION LANGUAGE

Canonical hero animation uses repeated face-down cards in a black void.

Composition:
- Cards occupy foreground, middle depth, and distance.
- Use varied scale, roll, pitch, and yaw.
- Include frontal cards, foreshortened cards, and thin edge-on slivers.
- Crop large foreground cards at frame boundaries.
- Use overlap to establish depth.
- Keep irregular black gaps between clusters.
- Avoid one regular spiral or evenly distributed wallpaper pattern.

Motion cues:
- Cards tumble or orbit through suspended space.
- A diagonal crimson energy slash may organize the frame.
- Small triangular shards and sparks reinforce direction.
- Motion looks controlled but turbulent.
- The repeated back emblem keeps chaos coherent.

Lighting:
- Emissive borders and emblems light nearby card planes.
- Cards near energy receive pink-red fill.
- Cards facing away remain charcoal and deep crimson.
- Foreground may use mild depth blur.
- Background stays black, not fog-filled scenery.
